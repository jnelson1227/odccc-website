Our story

How a group of carvers with chainsaws turned a timber town into the Chainsaw Carving Capital of Oregon.

Born from the working forest

Chainsaw carving grew out of the Pacific Northwest timber industry — the same tools that shaped towns like Reedsport, turned to art. It's a living craft, passed on through demonstration, competition and mentorship.

Every June, carvers from across the country and around the world bring that tradition back to Rainbow Plaza.

2000The first championshipReedsport natives Bob and Cindy King — Bob a championship carver himself — launch the event at Rainbow Plaza.
2011Best in OregonThe Oregon Festivals & Events Association names it Best Performing Art Festival in Oregon, and the Legislature designates Reedsport the Chainsaw Carving Capital of Oregon.
TodayA living galleryProduced by volunteers of the Reedsport/Winchester Bay Chamber of Commerce. Many sculptures are donated to the city and installed around town.
