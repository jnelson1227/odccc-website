Getting here

Find us

Rainbow Plaza, downtown Reedsport. Reedsport sits on Highway 101 where Oregon Route 38 meets the coast — halfway between Florence and Coos Bay.

At the championship

What to expect

Open booths

Every carver works in an open booth. Watch up close, ask questions, meet the artists.

Quick Carve

Daily at 10:30 a.m. — 90 minutes on the clock, a finished sculpture at the buzzer.

Live auctions

Take home a one-of-a-kind piece. Quick Carve sculptures sell at every evening auction.

Food & vendors

Local food, drinks and vendors on the plaza all four days.

Year-round

The sculpture trail

The championship never really leaves. Many finished sculptures are donated to the City of Reedsport and installed around town — turning Reedsport into a living, open-air gallery you can walk any day of the year.

Around Reedsport

Where to stay & eat — Visit Reedsport →

Oregon Dunes

The largest coastal dune system in North America — hiking, OHV riding and camping.

Dean Creek Elk

Roosevelt elk year-round, three miles east of town on Route 38.

Umpqua Discovery Center

Natural and cultural history of the lower Umpqua, right downtown.

Winchester Bay

Marina, fishing and crabbing where the Umpqua meets the Pacific.
